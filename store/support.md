## Common questions

**Online play says it can't reach the server.**
The game server sleeps when nobody is playing. The first connection can take up to a minute while it wakes up. Leave the screen open and it connects on its own.

**My opponent left.**
If a player disconnects, their seat is held for 30 seconds. If they don't come back, the game ends and you win by resignation.

**How do I play with a friend?**
Choose Play Multiplayer, create a private room and send your friend the six-character room code. They choose Join room and enter it.

**What are the rules?**
Standard American checkers, plus one chess knight per side. The knight moves like a chess knight, is never forced to capture, and has to rest for one turn after it moves. Tap "How to play" on the home screen for the full rules.
